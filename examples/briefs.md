# Briefs you can start with

These are synthetic examples. Replace the placeholders with your own content and supplied assets. They do not encode the creator’s personal briefs, identity or style.

## YouTube thumbnail

~~~text
Use BeyondArtist. My video explains [actual topic] for [audience]. Create one
YouTube thumbnail and propose a complementary title. Keep visible copy short.
Study this category, find a strong original angle and choose the art direction.
Use my attached portrait as an identity reference. Verify any factual hook.
~~~

## Instagram carousel

~~~text
Use BeyondArtist. Make an eight-slide Instagram carousel about [topic] for
[audience]. Use [language]. I want [tone] and short copy. Give the opening a
strong visual hook, vary the layouts and keep one coherent art/type system.
Show the finished slides in order and check readability on a phone.
~~~

## Freelance service campaign

~~~text
Use BeyondArtist. I sell [service] to [client type]. Create a visual campaign
explaining [verified benefit]. Use the attached brand rules. Help me choose
the creative angle, palette and hierarchy. I need [formats and quantities],
finished exports and editable files if the available tools support them.
~~~

## Phrase or character art

~~~text
Use BeyondArtist. Turn “[exact phrase]” into one expressive poster for [platform].
The emotion is [mood]. Use my supplied character references where they serve
the concept. Give the typography and artwork an intentional relationship.
Choose an original art form and inspect its craft before delivery.
~~~

## Complete brief

~~~text
Use BeyondArtist. Follow the attached exact structure and brand constraints.
Deliver the requested direction first, plus one distinct artist interpretation
within those constraints. Explain the difference briefly and show both.
~~~

## Bounded edit

~~~text
Use BeyondArtist. In the attached approved design, change only “[old copy]” to
“[new copy]”. Preserve the rest. Check the replacement and final export.
~~~
