## Count definitions

A set record is one visually reviewed image preview linked to its actual pin URL. Each final set has 100 distinct pin URLs and 100 distinct image-file identifiers. Across sets, URLs/image identifiers can repeat because related feeds overlap. The total is 700 study records covering 694 distinct pin URLs/image identifiers, not 700 wholly independent artworks. Shared assets, style series and related compositions are additionally noted by visual reviewers. Video stills and carousel covers are labeled as preview-level reviews. No inaccessible or blank item is credited as visually studied.

## Capture and review limitations

Related-feed samples were gathered from rendered, loaded page content, then studied in labeled contact sheets and individual views when needed. Early crops accidentally included clipped carousel siblings or neighboring cards; visual review detected and repaired or replaced them. Loading placeholders, mismatched fragments and selected duplicate references were corrected before the final count. The final three repairs in set 7 used larger main-image views of the same original pin URLs. The set ledgers refer to the corrected records. Distinct image variants within shared composition families are retained and explicitly noted; unique URLs do not prove independent underlying artworks.

Most references were inspected as thumbnail/preview images, not original artwork files. The study supports composition, broad palette, image/type relationships and visible hierarchy. It does not establish exact font files, color-managed swatches, tiny-copy accuracy, complete video behavior or unseen slide narratives. Source detail is marked uncertain rather than invented.

The failed original fourth URL and its replacement are documented in [the seed notes](pinterest-seeds.md). No other source URLs were invented to fill the count. Local captures remain private research intermediates and are not in the distributable skill.
