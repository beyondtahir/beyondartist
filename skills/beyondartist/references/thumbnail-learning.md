# Thumbnail learning for the designer and artist

The canonical [thumbnail studies](library/thumbnail-studies.md) contain saved visual analyses of completed thumbnail artwork and thumbnail portfolios. They were inspected through the connected Chrome browser, with dated Pinterest reaction evidence. This is written retrieval memory, not model-weight training.

## Admission and interpretation

The creator requested at least 100 qualifying reference pins. The current threshold is **50 likes or visible Pinterest heart reactions**; **100 saves may substitute when likes are not shown**. Preserve the exact visible metric label. A heart control labeled React is a reaction count, not a save count. A hidden count is unknown; zero is not unknown. Abbreviated counts must retain their raw display and approximate precision.

Rank only within the inspected, admitted pool. Search results are personalized and do not establish Pinterest's global most-liked thumbnails. Pinterest reactions, saves, portfolio claims and displayed video-view numbers do not establish YouTube CTR, causal design effectiveness or guaranteed virality. A multi-thumbnail portfolio is one metric-bearing pin; never assign its count to each panel. Distinct pin URLs alone do not establish distinct artwork. Exact duplicate artwork uses one preferred source with its strongest observed count and a duplicate-source trail.

Inspect the actual completed design, not just its title, tags or generated alt text. Exclude unfinished process frames, image assets, channel banners, unrelated posters and dimensions guides from the qualified-thumbnail count. Preserve rejected candidates in the audit. Record uncertain publication context without claiming that the artwork was used on a live YouTube video.

## Apply the saved studies

1. The designer defines the audience, truthful promise and emotional tension, then inspects current category examples when browsing works.
2. Retrieve two to four relevant full `THUMB-*` records by topic and mechanism. Include a useful contrast—for example, quiet environmental type against a confrontational portrait—when the brief is open. Read `FND-*` and `SYS-*` guidance for color, typography and hierarchy alongside them.
3. Write a decision table: **source ID → visible lesson → our original application → final inspection check**. Record what is observation and what is a hypothesis. Do not infer why people reacted merely from the count.
4. The artist compares at least three meaningfully different concepts for a major design. Change the visual metaphor, composition, emotion or relationship between the subject and text. A recolored copy is not a new concept.
5. Specify the primary, secondary and tertiary attention path. Decide exact copy, type voice, scale, line breaks, contrast and overlap before rendering. Make expression and gesture complete the message; use light and color to establish priority, not decoration.
6. Create with the strongest available image model and precise typography finishing where needed. Inspect at full and phone size. Check identity, truthful claims, spelling, fingers, visual integrations, clipped letters and competing highlights.

## Useful transferable mechanisms

- **Editorial confrontation:** a short claim and serious portrait create tension. Check the factual implication of arrows, color and juxtaposition; an attractive collage can still make a false allegation.
- **Physicalized problem:** one symbolic object makes an abstract process readable. Keep the object related to the promise and prevent detail from burying it.
- **Typographic argument:** scale, a strike or a word appearing behind the subject can express contrast. Preserve letter recognition and avoid a second competing headline.
- **Environmental calm:** a small person in a large setting and restrained lettering can express relief, distance or reflection. Calm is a deliberate option, not a weak version of a loud thumbnail.
- **Lifestyle collage:** tightly edited moments plus one headline create a diary feel. Vary image scale; equal-weight tiles and tiny decoration can undermine the hook.
- **Transformation proof:** before/after or sketch/final makes a change legible. Label the relationship honestly; study the final thumbnail separately from the portfolio wrapper.

These mechanisms summarize repeated observations in the saved collection; they are options to test against a brief, not a ranking of winning styles. Read the individual records before attributing a specific lesson.

## Research method and offline use

Use the authorized browser for Pinterest research. This collection does not require Apify, a scraper, an API key or a login at generation time. If live access fails, say that stored Design Memory is being used and continue within the creator's authorization. Do not say live Pinterest was checked when only saved notes were read.

The artist can apply the written lessons offline. Private browser screenshots support the research audit, but are not required by retrieval and are not bundled as reusable creative assets. Save source URLs, dated metric observations and explicit limits with any new study. Rebuild and validate the index after changing canonical records.
