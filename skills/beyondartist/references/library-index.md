# BeyondArtist Design Memory

Use this index for retrieval, then read relevant entries. The library contains written analysis that works offline; it is not model-weight training, licensed stock imagery or evidence of audience performance. Read observations as evidence and interpretations as judgments. All reference styles are optional; client-specific brand rules come from the client's brief.

For every new design, retrieve and apply relevant knowledge even though individual visual styles remain optional. Use [local retrieval](retrieval.md) to search the unified memory, read selected full entries with their context, and connect their lessons to concrete decisions. `SYS-*` records preserve creator workflow preferences; `FND-*`, `PIN-*`, `TIP-*` and `THUMB-*` preserve visual studies and their limitations. The original collection need not be uploaded again. Historical source names and checksums remain in provenance, not as the library's name.

## Important corrections and limits

- FND-009's 1080 × 1440 label is **3:4**, not 4:5. 1080 × 1350 is 4:5. Verify current destination specifications; source platform/reach/music/safe-zone claims are unverified.
- FND-027's Tangerine illustration looks orange but prints `#FFC300`, a conflicting amber/yellow label. Preserve the conflict rather than using it as an orange brand token.
- FND-042 prints “Keming”; the intended term is **kerning**. FND-040's “San Serif” should be **sans serif**.
- In later palettes “Silver” accompanies `#141414`; labels such as “Pistel Blue” and “Cyber Line” are source wording. “Lemon Chiffon” appears with differing codes. Treat informal names as labels, not a standard.
- Two fonts, three colors, ten slides and left alignment are contextual source suggestions. None is mandatory for all work; left alignment must not override right-to-left languages.
- The moodboard story FND-045–047 is incomplete. Palette-deck gaps and likely sequence order are documented per entry. Do not invent missing slides or results.
- The new boards use broad style labels. The “Neoclassical” board mixes contemporary collage and historical motifs; it is not an accurate art-history classification. Illustrative dashboard metrics in Bento Grid are not business facts.
- Colors/fonts not explicitly printed are visual descriptions. Captured UI arrows, save buttons, badges, clocks and playback controls are not part of the artwork.
- Third-party source logos, portraits, character art, image files and fonts are not licensed for production by being included as references. The distributable contains written analysis and citations only.
